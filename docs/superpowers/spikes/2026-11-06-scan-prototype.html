Seeing through fields.

Magnetic fields are everywhere and invisible. Two small magnets in a 5 by 5 metre room fill it with structure no eye can see.

scroll to measure

A quantum sensor measures the field, one slice at a time.

Each pass through the room records the field strength on a plane. The field itself is never seen, only its trace in the data.

Simulation and machine learning turn the data back into its cause.

From the measurements alone, Antaclis infers where the sources are, how strong they are, and which way they point.
